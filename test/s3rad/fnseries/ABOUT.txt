Fn series v1: 12 hydride plates, E635 property set
====================================================

Geometry (unchanged from the presentation series)
  test/s3rad/mkseeddeck.py --seed 5 --fn P --kn 1e6 --gmin 2e-6
  P = 0, 20, 25, 33.34, 40, 60, 80, 100  ->  0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 12 of 12
  plates radial (axis x = load = hoop direction), the rest tangential
  (axis y = tube radius).  Seed 5 reproduces the plate layout of the
  presentation (slide 5).  Meshed with gmsh 4.15.2.

Properties (tools/mkprops.py, applied to the decks above -> decks_v1/)
  ZR   E 100 GPa, nu 0.335                      Kim & Lee 2024, tab. 2
       sigma_y 490 MPa                          Fedotov et al., fig. 35
                                                (E635 rings, 293 K, unirradiated)
       sigma/sigma_y = 1.27 / 1.52 / 1.80 / 2.45 at eps 0.05 / 0.2 / 0.5 / 1.0
                                                Fedotov et al., fig. 33
       eps_f(eta) = old s3rad shape x 8.1,      Egorochkin 2023, tab. 3.1:
       eps_f(1/3) = 0.97                        dS/S = 0.62 for Fn < 0.25, 20 C
       u_f 0.015 mm (unchanged)
  ZRH  E 130 GPa, sigma_y 1160 MPa              Kim & Lee tab. 2; Chan et al. 2016
       eps_f(1/3) = 0.01 (cleavage)             Chan et al. 2016
       G_c = K_C^2 (1-nu^2)/E = 0.075 kJ/m2     K_C = 3.3 MPa m^0.5 (Chan)
  INTERFACE  G_c = 0.59 G_c(ZrH) = 0.044 kJ/m2  Chan fig. 3, load ratio 2.15/2.8
       Tn0 300 / Ts0 260 MPa, Kn 1e6 N/mm3 (unchanged; seed facet 275/240)
  Fracture energies x10: plate radius 0.3 mm against a ~60 um real radial
  hydride (Plyasov et al. 2023: > 50 um) - LEFM similitude.  u_f(ZrH) =
  2 G_c / sigma_y = 1.29e-3 mm; interface G_c 0.44 kJ/m2.
  Step: grip displacement 2.0 mm (50 % nominal), same displacement increments.

Run (tools/run_one.sh, tools/run_series.sh)
  solver: this branch, src/ccx_2.23_pardiso built with Makefile.ubuntu2404.mkl
  flags of the disc series (fractrure-tests/runs/run.sh), 1 thread per run,
  4 runs at a time.  The scripts carry the container paths they ran with
  (/home/user/fn-series, /home/user/ccx-fn).

Results (results_v1/, refreshed while the series runs; tools/saver.sh)
  curve.csv   eps (grip u / 4 mm), sigma (RF_x / 5.76 mm2)
  sta.txt, cvg.txt, damage.txt, events.txt, run_tail.txt, provenance.txt
  series.txt  peak, eps at peak, eps where force fell to 10 % of peak, work
  The .frd fields are not in git (size).

rings_E635_Egorochkin_2023.csv: ring tension of hydrided E635 tubes,
  elongation delta and fracture-area reduction against Fn (thesis tab. 3.1).
