Animations for the presentation (Manim Community 0.19.1, manim-slides 5.7)

  fn_loop.gif        Fn 0 -> 100 -> 0 %, the 12 plates of the series (seed 5)
  deck_of_cards.gif  delta platelets on the alpha basal plane stack into a
                     mesoscale hydride at 14.7 deg, (10-17) (Motta 2019)
  texture.gif        basal poles R -> T turn the hydrides tangential -> radial;
                     (0001) pole figure, Fn(phi) by the 45 deg rule, f_R
  reorient.gif       stress reorientation: dissolve at 400 C, cool with and
                     without hoop stress (sigma_th ~ 45 MPa, Plyasov 2023)

Each GIF loops without a seam, 1600x900, 25 fps, black background; one GIF
per slide, the top ~12 % left empty for the slide title, Noto Serif (the
deck's font), labels sized for the back of a hall (style.py).
reorient.gif shows one stress cycle only and returns to the start by an
explicit, labelled cross-fade (no claim about stress-free re-cooling, where
the memory effect can keep hydrides radial).
Render:  manim -r 1600,900 --fps 25 <scene>.py <Class>;  mkgif.sh in.mp4 out.gif 1600
demo_fn.py + make_decks.py: click-stepped decks (video / GIF / PDF).
