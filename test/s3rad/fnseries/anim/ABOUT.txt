Animations for the presentation (Manim Community 0.19.1, manim-slides 5.7)

  fn_loop.gif        the 12 plates of the series turn radial one by one and
                     back; the formula F_n = k/12 = ... follows (seed 5)
  deck_of_cards.gif  delta platelets on the alpha basal plane stack into a
                     mesoscale hydride at 14.7 deg, (10-17) (Motta 2019)
  texture.gif        basal poles R -> T turn the hydrides tangential -> radial;
                     (0001) pole figure, R at the centre
  reorient.gif       stress reorientation, qualitative: heat (hydrides
                     dissolve), load, cool under load, unload - radial
                     hydrides stay

Each GIF loops without a seam, 1600x900, 25 fps, black background; one GIF
per slide, the top band (y > 2.85) left empty for the slide title, Noto Serif
(the deck's font).  No numbers beyond F_n and 14.7 deg: the GIFs show the
meaning, the slide text gives the data.

Layout (style.py): every element is placed from measured bounding boxes in a
grid inside CONTENT (x -6.71..6.71, y -3.65..2.85); report() prints overlaps
and anything outside, checkframes.py checks rendered frames for ink in the
title band and the margins.  The 3D cell in deck_of_cards.py is sized and
centred from the camera projection of its vertices over the whole turn.
Type scale at 1600x900: L1 MathTex 64 (cap 51 px), L2 Text 30 / MathTex 44,
L3 Text 26 / MathTex 38 - nothing smaller.

reorient.gif shows one stress cycle only and returns to the start by an
explicit, labelled cross-fade (no claim about stress-free re-cooling, where
the memory effect can keep hydrides radial).

Render:  manim -r 1600,900 --fps 25 <scene>.py <Class>;  mkgif.sh in.mp4 out.gif 1600
         python3 checkframes.py media/videos/<scene>/900p25/<scene>.mp4
demo_fn.py + make_decks.py: click-stepped decks (video / GIF / PDF), older.
